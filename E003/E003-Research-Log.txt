    Research Question:
        - Does training the model longer improve generation quality?

        - Training Tokens -> 300M

        - Layers -> 6

        - Block size -> 256
        - Dropout Rate -> 0.05
        - Vocabulary -> 50304

        - Tokenizer -> Tiktoken GPT2
        - Batch Size -> 32

        - Trainable parameters: 30,062,592

    # Comparison with Experiment 002:

        - No visible evidence of overfitting
        - Training remained similar
        - Training Loss reduced over time due to longer training


    # Generation observation
        - Improved grammatical fluency comparaed with previous version
        - Longer text still remain readbale

        - Long story genearation still remains the biggest limitation

    # Current Conclusions
        - The corrected configuration produces slightly better validation results
        - It also appears to produce more fluent long-form samples

        - However, the difference in genearation quality has not yet been established through a control evaluation
